03 / THE REQUESTING AGENCY VIEW

Ask for an answer, not the records.

Source details withheld

An authorized agency would choose a property and an approved question. It would not browse the underlying filing.

Does this filing report at least 20 rent-regulated residential units?

A fixed question limits what is disclosed. Additional questions would need their own approval and implementation.

▧

The filing stays behind.

The agency would not receive the exact unit count, income lines, expense details, or original filing.

It would receive the approved answer and the evidence needed to check it.

What could this support?

An agency could use a narrow fact to inform an initial review or request for follow-up. The answer would describe what was reported; it would not establish legal status, confirm the owner’s accuracy, or decide eligibility by itself.

THE IDEA TO TAKE AWAY

Share the fact needed for a decision.
Keep the rest of the filing protected.

DOF retains

The source filing and private details used to answer the question.

The agency receives

An approved answer, permitted source context, and, in the target product, a checkable proof.

The proof establishes

That the answer follows from the committed reported values. It does not establish that the report is true.
